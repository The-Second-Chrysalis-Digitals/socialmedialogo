# Social Photo Exporter Icon

Created for Susana Quintal's Social Photo Exporter with the built-in image generation tool.

Final assets:

- `social-photo-exporter-v2-source.png`: original generated bitmap
- `social-photo-exporter-v2-32.png`: browser favicon
- `social-photo-exporter-v2-180.png`: Apple touch icon
- `social-photo-exporter-v2-192.png`: app manifest and header
- `social-photo-exporter-v2-512.png`: app manifest and downloadable icon

The installation sizes are downscaled from the original without changing the design. Earlier icons are retained.

## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished square app icon for the existing 'Social Photo Exporter' app, created by Susana Quintal. Create ONE single polished icon, not a presentation sheet or mockup. Solid full-bleed Blackberry Plum background #4A2F58. Center a distinctive bold geometric emblem combining two overlapping photo frames (a back landscape frame and a front portrait frame) with a clean upward/right export arrow integrated into the top right of the front frame. Use crisp white for the outlines and arrow, and restrained deep teal #176D73 plus bright mint #70D7C6 for a simple mountain/photo shape inside. The photo shape should be broad and geometric, not detailed scenery. Flat vector-like graphic rendered as a high-quality bitmap, strong contrast, optical balance, professional but friendly. No text, letters, initials, APCM logo, watermark, gradients, shadows, lighting effects, spheres or decorative blobs. The icon should fill the central 70 percent of a square canvas with generous clear margins suitable for OS masking and remain legible at 32 pixels. Do not draw a rounded outer tile; the background fills the entire square.
